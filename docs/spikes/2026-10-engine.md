# Spike: MLVFS on FUSE-T 1.2.9 (arm64)

Clip M29-0615.MLV (320 frames, 1600x2040, LJ92), `--resolve-naming --bad-pix -f`, full sequential read of every DNG
through the mount (~6.6 MB/frame), Apple Silicon, fresh mount per run.

## Backend selection
FUSE-T wiki lists mount option `backend=[smb|nfs|fskit]`; NFS is default; no env var documented.
`-o backend=smb` works through mlvfs (verified: `mount` shows `smbfs` vs `nfs` for default).

## Results
| Backend | Clip | Frames | fps | MB/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFS (default) | M29-0615 | 320 | 92.7 / 99.8 / 100.2 | 611 / 658 / 661 |
| NFS | M23-1246 | 539 | 92.3 | 608 |
| SMB | M29-0615 | 320 | 133.4 | 879 |
| SMB | M23-1249 | 514 | 120.8 | 797 |

All passes: DNG counts 320/539/514 as expected, IFD0 1600x2040, unmount leaves empty mount point, no stale mounts.
Both backends exceed the 25 fps (real-time) target by 4-5x, so no fallback needed. SMB is ~30% faster here.

## Findings
- Resolve-naming folder: `M29-0615MLV_1_2026-08-29_0001_C0000/`, DNGs `<folder>_000000.dng`... (not `<clip>/`).
- Mounting a folder exposes one folder per MLV in it (plus subdirs of the folder).
- Mount ready in 0.2-0.5 s. mlvfs printed nothing on stderr.
- Not measured: Resolve's own access pattern (random/parallel reads), cold disk cache.
